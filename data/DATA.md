# Data

Files `data/<name>/<name>.csv` (helpdesk, BPIC2012_A, BPIC2012_O, BPIC2012_W), as shipped in the MTLFormer repository.

- Helpdesk: Verenich, I.: Helpdesk. Mendeley Data V1 (2016), https://doi.org/10.17632/39bp3vv62t.1 (21,348 events)
- BPIC2012: van Dongen, B.: BPI Challenge 2012, 4TU.ResearchData, https://doi.org/10.4121/uuid:3926db30-f712-4394-aebc-75976070e91f, split by the initial letter of the activity name into A, O and W.

The shipped BPIC2012 CSVs differ from the public XES logs (A 73,022 vs 60,849 events, O 41,728 vs 31,244, W 147,450 vs 72,413; minute timestamps; duplicated rows). Licences follow the dataset sources.
